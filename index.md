# Privacy Policy – Kasino – Nordic Card Game

**Last updated:** 2025  
**Developer:** O-P Card House

## Introduction
O-P Card House respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the mobile game **Kasino – Nordic Card Game**.

## Information We Collect
Kasino – Nordic Card Game does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- No personal information is collected
- No account registration required
- No analytics or advertising services used
- No data is transmitted to external servers

## Game Data
All game-related data is stored locally on the user’s device only.

## Third-Party Services
This game does not use third-party services that collect personal data.

## Children’s Privacy
This game does not collect personal data from children under 13.
