# Privacy Policy – O-P Card House Games

Last updated: 2026
Developer: O-P Card House
Contact: [opcardhouse@opsahkopalvelut.fi](mailto:opcardhouse@opsahkopalvelut.fi)

## Introduction

O-P Card House respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use O-P Card House mobile games.

This Privacy Policy applies to games published by O-P Card House, including Kasino – Nordic Card Game and other O-P Card House games.

## Information We Collect

Our games do not require account registration.

We do not collect directly identifying personal information such as email address, phone number, home address, payment information, or precise location.

Some games may use Firebase services provided by Google to enable online game features.

When online features are used, the following game-related data may be sent to Firebase:

* player nickname or display name entered by the user
* game cards and game state
* player scores
* online room or match information needed for the game to work

This information is used only to provide the online game functionality.

## How We Use Information

The collected game-related data is used to:

* create and join online game rooms
* synchronize the game between players
* show player nicknames during the game
* update cards, turns, and scores during online matches
* maintain basic game functionality

We do not sell personal data.

We do not use this data for advertising.

## Game Data

Some game data is stored locally on the user’s device.

When online features are used, necessary game data may be transmitted to and stored in Firebase cloud services so that the online game can work.

The online game data may remain in Firebase for the time needed to provide the game features and maintain the service.

## Third-Party Services

The app may use Firebase, a service provided by Google.

Firebase may process game-related data as necessary to provide the technical online features of the app.

Firebase privacy and security information is available from Google’s Firebase documentation.

## Analytics and Advertising

Our games do not use advertising services.

Our games do not use Firebase Analytics unless this is clearly stated separately for a specific app version.

## Children’s Privacy

Our games are designed to be suitable for families and children.

We do not knowingly collect directly identifying personal information from children.

If online features are available, users should not enter their real name, email address, phone number, address, or other personal information as their nickname.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We do not share game-related data with advertisers.

Game-related data may be processed by Firebase/Google only as necessary to provide the online features of the app.

## Security

We use Firebase services to provide online features. Firebase security rules and technical protections are used to help protect the game data.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future.
