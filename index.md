# Privacy Policy – O-P Card House Games

Last updated: 2026
Developer: O-P Card House
Contact: [opcardhouse@gmail.com](mailto:opcardhouse@gmail.com)

## Introduction

O-P Card House respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use O-P Card House mobile games, including Kasino – Nordic Card Game and Kertolaskut 1–10.

## Information We Collect

Our games do not require account registration and do not collect directly identifying personal information such as real name, email address, phone number, address, or payment information.

Depending on the game and its features, the app may use Firebase services provided by Google to enable online features, game synchronization, and app functionality.

When Firebase is used, the following information may be processed:

* player nickname or display name, if the user enters one
* online room or match information
* gameplay data needed for multiplayer or online features
* technical information required for Firebase services to work, such as device, app, and connection-related data

## How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide and maintain the game features, such as online multiplayer, game rooms, synchronization between players, and basic app functionality.

We do not sell personal data.

## Game Data

Some game data may be stored locally on the user’s device.

If online features are used, necessary game data may be transmitted to and stored in Firebase cloud services so that the online features can work.

## Third-Party Services

The app may use Firebase, a service provided by Google.

Firebase may process data as described in Google’s Firebase privacy and security documentation.

## Analytics and Advertising

The games do not use advertising services.

The games do not use Firebase Analytics unless clearly stated separately in the app version.

## Children’s Privacy

Our games are designed to be suitable for families and children. We do not knowingly collect directly identifying personal information from children.

If online features are available, users should not enter real names or other personal information as player nicknames.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers.

Data may be processed by Firebase/Google only as necessary to provide the app’s technical features.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future.
